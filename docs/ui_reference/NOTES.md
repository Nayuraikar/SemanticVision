STYLE REFERENCE ONLY. Unrelated project (a walkable ancient-city explorer). Never copy its assets, branding or design; copy the FEEL.
- The live 3D scene is always on screen. Menus are glass cards that fade in over the dimmed, blurred scene, not separate pages.
- Choosing something flies the camera to it with eased motion. No hard cuts.
- Warm cinematic light: golden-hour tone, soft bloom, glowing lamps/emissives, long shadows, drifting ambient particles.
- Glowing waypoint markers (ring on the ground + beacon) guide where to look or go next.
- Minimal HUD: rounded pill buttons, quality selector, minimap, time-of-day slider that visibly relights the scene.
- Direct manipulation: drag to orbit, hover glows, click-to-focus, smooth easing everywhere.
- Calm and polished, never cluttered. Few controls on screen at once; the rest live in drawers.
